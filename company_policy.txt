# Company Employee Policy

## Working Hours
Employees are expected to work from Monday to Friday, from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Employees should arrive on time and inform their manager if they are running late.

## Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 20 annual leaves per year and 10 sick leaves per year. Leave requests must be submitted to the manager at least two days in advance, except in emergencies.

## Remote Work Policy
Employees can work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval. They must remain available during official working hours.

## Attendance Policy
Employees must mark their attendance daily. Repeated late arrivals without a valid reason may result in disciplinary action.

## Employee Benefits
The company provides health insurance, paid annual leaves, professional development opportunities, and performance-based bonuses.

## Code of Conduct
Employees must maintain professional behavior, respect colleagues, protect confidential company information, and follow company policies.

## Contact Information
For HR-related questions, employees can contact the Human Resources department at hr@company.com.
